Privacy Policy for Deep Focus

1. Introduction

Deep Focus is a productivity and focus timer application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

a) Firebase Analytics (Google)

  • App usage data (screens visited, session duration, events)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Approximate location (country/region level)
  • App version and build info

b) Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Device state at time of crash (memory, OS, app version)
  • Stack traces (no personal data)

c) Firebase Performance Monitoring (Google)

  • App startup time
  • Network request performance
  • Screen rendering performance

3. Sound Library

Deep Focus includes a built-in Sound Library with focus sounds and nature soundscapes (e.g., White Noise, Rain, Ocean, Birds, Piano). All audio files are bundled directly within the app as local assets.

  • No audio is streamed from external servers or third-party music services.
  • No internet connection is required to play sounds.
  • Your sound selection and playback are handled entirely on your device.
  • We do not collect, store, or share any data related to which sounds you listen to or how long you play them.

4. How We Use the Data

The collected data is used solely to:

  • Improve app stability by identifying and fixing crashes
  • Understand how users interact with the App to improve features
  • Monitor and improve app performance

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is processed by Google Firebase in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics data may be retained according to the retention settings and policies of these services. We use this information only for purposes such as understanding app usage, improving the application, and diagnosing technical issues.

6. Data Deletion

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly. Therefore, there are no user accounts or account data that users need to delete.

Data collected automatically through third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics is managed according to the applicable settings and policies of those services.

7. Children's Privacy

Deep Focus does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided data, contact us and we will address it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Deep Focus does not require account creation and does not collect any personally identifiable information. All data collected through Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring is anonymous and cannot be linked to any individual user.

Because no personal data is tied to you, there is nothing to access, export, or delete on an individual basis.

If you wish to opt out of analytics tracking, you may uninstall the App at any time.
